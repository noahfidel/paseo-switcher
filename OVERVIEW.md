Arc-style workspace switching for the Paseo desktop app. Hold Cmd and tap ` (the default shortcut) to step through your most recently used workspaces, shown as a list with each one's full name, branch, pull request and agent status. Release Cmd to switch. A quick tap of the shortcut flips straight back to the previous workspace without drawing anything. Start typing while it is open to find any workspace by name, project, branch or pull request number.

## Keys

- Cmd+` / Cmd+Shift+`: open the switcher and move forward or back (or Ctrl+Tab, Option+Tab or Ctrl+`, see the Shortcut setting). With Ctrl+Tab or Option+Tab, Ctrl+` or Option+` also moves back while the switcher is open, and forward with Shift; while it is closed, the press is left to Paseo
- Release the modifier (Cmd, or Ctrl or Option for the other shortcuts): switch to the selected workspace
- Arrow keys move, Enter switches, Esc cancels
- → on a workspace (↓ in the Thumbnails layout) lists its sessions in place: its chats (the one you last had open there first, then by last change, each with its provider and status), then its terminals in Paseo's order. Move as usual, then release the modifier, press Enter or click to open that chat or terminal; ← goes back to the workspaces.
- Typing filters all workspaces (or a workspace's sessions by chat title or terminal name) and keeps the switcher open until Enter or a click; Esc clears the filter
- Hovering a row selects it and clicking switches to it
- ⌫ with nothing typed archives the selected workspace in Paseo, after a second ⌫ to confirm (see below); on a plugin screen, which Paseo cannot archive, it hides the screen instead. ⇧⌫, or the × on a hovered row, hides a workspace or plugin screen from the switcher (see below). Inside a workspace's sessions, ⌫ archives the selected chat or closes the selected terminal, always after a second ⌫ (the hint says "⌫ again to archive" or "⌫ again to close"); the × on a hovered chat or terminal follows the same rule. With text typed, ⌫ erases it (Ctrl or Alt erases it all)

The Command Center also has **Switch workspace (Cmd+`)**, which (its title shows the shortcut you chose) opens the same switcher and waits for Enter or a click. Use it in a browser tab, where the browser keeps Ctrl+Tab for itself, or in a phone browser, where a tap switches.

## Settings

**Settings → Plugins → Workspace Switcher** has seven settings, saved on each device along with the visit history:

- **Layout**: **List** (the default) shows each workspace's full name, pull request and checks, unpushed commits, and what its agent is doing and for how long, plus the project, branch, host and diff stat as the sidebar's Display settings show them. It takes no thumbnails, so the plugin's only background work is noting which chat tab you are on and, with messages counted as visits, refreshing the agent list every 5 seconds in idle time. **Thumbnails** shows a row of cards with a preview of each workspace instead.
- **Shortcut**: **Cmd+`** (the default), **Ctrl+Tab**, **Option+Tab** or **Ctrl+`**. Hold the modifier, tap the key to open and step forward, add Shift to step back, and release the modifier to switch. On macOS, turn off the system's "Move focus to next window" shortcut (System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Keyboard), which otherwise keeps Cmd+` for itself. Ctrl+Tab suits Windows and Linux; macOS keeps it for switching apps. Paseo uses Ctrl+` itself (Toggle Explorer sidebar), so Ctrl+` is only for when you have unbound that. Keys are matched by physical position, so the shortcut works on any keyboard layout.
- **Workspaces shown**: how many recent workspaces appear, 2 to 9, default 5.
- **Include plugin screens**: on by default. Plugin screens you open (a board, Bots, Schedules) become stops in the list, shown with their own icon (the one on their highlighted sidebar row, else the one beside their header title) and mixed with workspaces by last visit and counted in the number shown. A screen counts the moment it opens; paseo-bots' pass-through "Opening chat" screen never does. Choosing one reopens it with the params it last had. Off, none are listed or remembered.
- **Include background workspaces**: on by default. Also lists workspaces hidden from the sidebar, marked "hidden". Your sidebar is unchanged. Hosts that do not support background workspaces keep the sidebar's list.
- **Count chats you message as visits**: on by default. A workspace you send a message to ranks as if you had just opened it, even when you messaged it from a screen outside it, such as a bot chat. Chats a paseo-bots routine or another bot starts do not count until you reply to them. Off, only opening a workspace counts. Hidden workspaces still appear only with **Include background workspaces** on.
- **Repo icons**: on by default. Each workspace shows its project's icon in place of the branch or folder icon. The plugin's daemon side looks for an icon file in the repo the way Paseo does for its sidebar (a favicon, icon or logo in `public/`, `static/`, `assets/` and similar folders, square, up to 64 KB). With none, and an `origin` remote on GitHub, it fetches that account's public avatar from `https://github.com/<owner>.png`, without credentials, at most once a day per account while Paseo runs (again after a daemon restart). Other remotes are never contacted. Off, nothing is read or fetched.

A workspace shows the avatar of the paseo-bots bot behind its most recently active chat that carries a bot label (bot workspaces hold only bot chats) in place of its repo icon, branch or folder icon. Bot avatars appear when paseo-bots is installed and enabled; without it nothing changes.

**Archiving.** The first ⌫ on a workspace, chat or terminal marks it in red and the hint says "⌫ again to archive"; a second ⌫ on that row archives it in Paseo, which for a worktree workspace removes its folder. Moving, typing, any other key or four seconds disarms it. Holding ⌫ never archives rows (only a fresh press acts), and a second ⌫ counts only after the first was let go and a quarter second has passed. The row goes at once and the selection stays on the same place, and the switcher stays open until Enter or a click (as when typing), so releasing the modifier does not open the row that moved up; if Paseo refuses, the row comes back and the hint line says why for a few seconds. Archiving goes through the host the workspace, chat or terminal belongs to. When a workspace's last session is archived or closed, the list returns to the workspaces.

**Hidden from switcher.** Hiding a row (⇧⌫, or the × on hover) means it is no longer listed, never cycled to by the shortcut or the quick tap, and records no visits, including messages. Typing a filter still finds hidden workspaces, and visiting one does not unhide it. Settings shows a **Hidden from switcher** section, only while something is hidden, with an **Unhide** button for each. (This is separate from the "hidden" tag, which marks workspaces Paseo hides from its sidebar.)

Switching to a workspace that has no sidebar row (a hidden one, or one in a collapsed project) reopens whichever is newer: the chat you last had open there (including one opened on the Bots screen), or the chat you last sent a message to (including one used from the Bots screen). If neither exists, it opens the most recently active chat that was not started by a routine, ask or PR review watcher. Workspaces in the sidebar open the way a sidebar click does.

## Cards

The switcher shows your most recently visited workspaces, where a visit is opening a workspace, opening one of its chats on paseo-bots' Bots screen, or, by default, sending it a message. Until you have visited enough of them, the rest fill in by latest activity. With several hosts, workspaces from every host the app can reach are listed together; a recent one whose host is offline keeps its name. Agent status follows Paseo's sidebar: a turning ring while an agent runs, amber when it needs input, red when it failed, and green when it finished and you have not looked yet.

In the Thumbnails layout, thumbnails are small images of the workspace view, focused on the conversation. While you stay in a workspace, the plugin refreshes its thumbnail every couple of seconds when the browser is idle. It never does this work while you are switching. Thumbnails are kept in memory only, so a workspace you have not opened since the app started shows "No preview yet" instead. Terminals, embedded previews and images loaded from the web appear blank in thumbnails.

## Limits

This plugin reads Paseo's page structure, which is not a public plugin API. It is pinned to Paseo 0.15 and may need an update when Paseo changes. It does nothing on iOS and Android.
